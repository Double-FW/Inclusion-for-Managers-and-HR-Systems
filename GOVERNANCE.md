# Governance for adoption

Version 0.1.0 is a review draft. Roles below are functions to appoint, not newly appointed people or spending authority. The organisation must approve scope, owners, protected time, resources, review intervals and any policy rules it adopts. AI may prepare material but cannot approve its own work or make employment decisions.

| Activity | Accountable role | Responsible role | Consult before adoption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose, scope and funding | Executive sponsor | Knowledge-base owner | HR, accessibility, lived experience |
| Practical guidance and editorial quality | Knowledge-base owner | Editorial maintainer | Role users and lived-experience contributors |
| Employment legal content | Designated legal/HR authority | Qualified legal reviewer | Jurisdiction specialists |
| Data and AI safeguards | Privacy authority | Privacy/AI service owners | Security, staff representatives, accessibility |
| Systems and procurement guidance | Service leadership | Product/procurement owners | HR, IT, accessibility, legal |
| Physical and emergency access | Facilities leadership | Facilities/safety owners | Affected people and safety expertise |
| Repository release | Designated release owner | Maintainer | All relevant reviewers |

## Participation and review

Include disabled, Deaf and neurodivergent people in priorities, drafting and evaluation with accessible participation and fair compensation. Do not require an employee to disclose or represent a whole population. Provide a route to disagree privately and record how findings changed the material. Training and review are working activities needing allocated time.

Proposed intervals are annual full content review and quarterly checks of legal/funding/AI dependencies, with immediate triage after significant change or reported harm. Confirm these locally; they are not statutory deadlines. Each adopted module needs owner, revision, reviewer, scope, review date and due date. Review upstream diffs manually and record affected claims.

## Release control

Technical validation permits a review package to be assembled; it does not authorise policy adoption or public legal claims. Record pending legal review, source/rights gaps, accessibility evaluation, staff review and licence decision in the release register. Release owners can publish a clearly scoped draft only if rights permit and its limitations are visible. Do not falsely mark an unresolved gate passed.

## Confidential cases

Use approved HR, reporting, union and legal routes for real cases. This repository is not a substitute for those services. Steer contributors away from identifiable case material and follow the incident route if it is submitted. An inclusion steering group coordinates and challenges; it does not take over confidential decisions without authority.
