# Trial AI as optional support

**Workflow:** W5 · **Status:** Draft operational guidance · **Coordinator:** ai service owner

**Trigger:** A person chooses to explore AI for a defined work barrier.

## Start with

- Person-chosen goal and accessible non-AI alternative
- Approved product, minimum data and permission scope
- Success, failure, review and rollback criteria

## 1. Define an optional trial

**Owner:** line manager

Agree task, benefit, duration and ability to decline or stop without penalty.

**Evidence before moving on:** A specific goal and non-AI support remain available.

## 2. Review product and data

**Owner:** privacy owner

Check accessible interface, data flows, security, retention, procurement and minimum necessary information.

**Evidence before moving on:** Approved scope using synthetic or authorised minimum data.

## 3. Bound actions

**Owner:** ai service owner

Use read-only access first where suitable; require explicit authority for actions with logs and recovery.

**Evidence before moving on:** Permissions distinguish drafting, sending and approving.

## 4. Run and verify

**Owner:** ai service owner

Verify summaries, transcription and actions against sources; assess accuracy, access, effort, privacy and control.

**Evidence before moving on:** Observed benefit and errors; no unsupported success claim.

## 5. Stop, adjust or continue

**Owner:** line manager

Review with the participant. Stop on exposure, unverified action, ineffective support or withdrawal. Restore the accessible alternative.

**Evidence before moving on:** Decision and next review recorded without profiling.

## Escalation

Escalate data exposure to security/privacy and employment decisions to authorised HR/hiring owners. Do not let an agent approve its own expansion of scope.

## Boundaries

No diagnosis, masking, emotion or personality inference; no autonomous hiring, disciplinary, promotion or adjustment-refusal decision.

## Review and closure

Agree a review date, check the outcome with the affected person, and reopen ineffective arrangements. Record the outcome, remaining work, continuing support and accessible challenge route. No automatic closure on equipment purchase or form completion.
