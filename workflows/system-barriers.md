# Investigate an inaccessible system or benefit

**Workflow:** W2 · **Status:** Draft operational guidance · **Coordinator:** service owner

**Trigger:** Someone cannot achieve a work or benefit goal, or an audit identifies exclusion.

## Start with

- Goal and failed task; environment and interaction method
- Impact, frequency and confidentiality needs
- Reproduction evidence using synthetic data

## 1. Capture the barrier

**Owner:** service owner

Record observable failure with minimum data and a reproducible task; recognise urgent pay, health or safety impacts.

**Evidence before moving on:** Goal, impact and affected route understood.

## 2. Provide an effective interim route

**Owner:** service owner

Preserve confidentiality and timeliness. Do not assume a colleague handling private data is acceptable.

**Evidence before moving on:** Person can access the essential service while repair continues.

## 3. Route responsibility

**Owner:** procurement owner

Internal: product/engineering owner. Supplier: procurement/vendor management plus service owner. Benefits: commissioning HR owner.

**Evidence before moving on:** Named delivery owner and update date.

## 4. Investigate the task

**Owner:** accessibility lead

Use representative walkthroughs and voluntary observation; evaluate effort, time, errors and recovery with uncertainty and no employee ranking.

**Evidence before moving on:** Task evidence and cumulative burden, not individual ability scores.

## 5. Agree and deliver repair

**Owner:** service owner

Record fix plan, supplier dependencies and interim access. Procurement/legal own contractual remedies and payment decisions.

**Evidence before moving on:** Repair and verification plan agreed; no unauthorised payment threat.

## 6. Verify the full goal

**Owner:** accessibility lead

Retest the affected workflow and assistive technology, including authentication, support and exports; obtain user feedback.

**Evidence before moving on:** Close only after the intended service is usable; reopen regressions.

## Escalation

Escalate prolonged or severe exclusion to service leadership and HR; procurement/legal assess contract remedies. Do not wait for exhaustive measurement before providing urgent access.

## Boundaries

No automatic finding of discrimination or breach; no managerial or agentic withholding of payment.

## Review and closure

Agree a review date, check the outcome with the affected person, and reopen ineffective arrangements. Record the outcome, remaining work, continuing support and accessible challenge route. No automatic closure on equipment purchase or form completion.

## Machine-use boundary

The structured file records the main sequence and conditional routes. It is human decision support, not an executable authority to decide a case. A refusal, failed trial, urgent risk or disputed decision requires the named human route; do not blindly advance to closure. `escalate` means route to the specified role while preserving support; `close` does not erase continuing arrangements or a challenge route.
