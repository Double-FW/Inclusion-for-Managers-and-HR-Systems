# Practical workflows

| Trigger | Workflow | Intended outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A support request or observed barrier | [Adjustments](adjustments.md) | Effective support with ownership and review |
| An inaccessible tool, process or benefit | [System barriers](system-barriers.md) | Timely and confidential access with permanent repair |
| A performance, attendance or conduct concern | [Fair performance](fair-performance.md) | Relevant evidence, accessible procedures and fair judgement |
| A shared activity or process excludes someone | [Team solutions](team-solutions.md) | Chosen, dignified participation and transferable learning |
| A person wants to investigate AI support | [Optional AI trials](ai-trials.md) | Useful support with control, verification and recovery |

These are descriptions of human work, not software instructions. Read each action with its boundary, responsible role and review questions. Urgent access, safety, privacy or harassment concerns may require immediate action through the appropriate organisational route.
