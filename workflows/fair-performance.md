# Address a performance, attendance or conduct concern fairly

**Workflow:** W3 · **Status:** Draft operational guidance · **Coordinator:** hr

**Trigger:** An observable work, attendance or conduct concern is raised.

## Start with

- Justified expectation and observable evidence
- Access, training, workload and adjustment delivery
- Employee response and applicable procedure

## 1. Describe the concern

**Owner:** line manager

Use actual work evidence, not personality, eye contact or speculation about health.

**Evidence before moving on:** Specific expectation and concern stated accessibly.

## 2. Check organisational barriers

**Owner:** line manager

Check tools, instructions, training, workload and whether agreed support was delivered.

**Evidence before moving on:** Facts separated from assumptions; access failures identified.

## 3. Hear the person

**Owner:** hr

Give usable information, preparation and response routes; consider support or accompaniment where applicable.

**Evidence before moving on:** Response, disputed facts and requested support recorded privately.

## 4. Correct access and agree a fair plan

**Owner:** line manager

Repair barriers and set sustainable expectations, support and review. Do not delay urgent harassment or safety action.

**Evidence before moving on:** Actions have owners, evidence and review dates.

## 5. Use a fair formal route if needed

**Owner:** hr

Check current rights, jurisdiction, procedure access and decision authority; give reasons and independent appeal.

**Evidence before moving on:** Consequential decision is human, authorised and challengeable.

## 6. Review outcomes and learning

**Owner:** hr

Check whether arrangements worked and the decision process was accessible; record corrections in approved systems.

**Evidence before moving on:** Closure includes outstanding support and challenge routes.

## Escalation

Use HR/legal for serious, disputed or rights-sensitive decisions; a person can use an independent route where their manager is implicated.

## Boundaries

Investigating barriers does not excuse misconduct or prove all concerns are disability-related. Actual case data stays outside GitHub.

## Review and closure

Agree a review date, check the outcome with the affected person, and reopen ineffective arrangements. Record the outcome, remaining work, continuing support and accessible challenge route. No automatic closure on equipment purchase or form completion.

## Machine-use boundary

The structured file records the main sequence and conditional routes. It is human decision support, not an executable authority to decide a case. A refusal, failed trial, urgent risk or disputed decision requires the named human route; do not blindly advance to closure. `escalate` means route to the specified role while preserving support; `close` does not erase continuing arrangements or a challenge route.
