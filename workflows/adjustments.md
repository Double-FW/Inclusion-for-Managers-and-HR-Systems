# Remove a barrier and review an adjustment

**Workflow:** W1 · **Status:** Draft operational guidance · **Coordinator:** line manager

**Trigger:** A request, observed difficulty or change in circumstances.

## Start with

- A private account of the task, barrier and preferred support
- Any urgent access, safety or privacy impact
- Existing arrangements and what may be shared

## 1. Receive and acknowledge

**Owner:** line manager

Accept any usable route; do not wait for a form, diagnosis or legal terminology. Ask privately about work and preferred support.

**Evidence before moving on:** A named coordinator and next update date; no diagnosis required.

## 2. Provide interim access

**Owner:** line manager

Address urgent access and safe cover; agree minimum necessary sharing. Involve the service owner where needed.

**Evidence before moving on:** Interim support, escalation and dependencies recorded.

## 3. Explore options

**Owner:** process owner

Consider tools, methods, environment and working arrangements with the person and relevant owners. Explain why any functional advice is needed.

**Evidence before moving on:** Options, impacts and the person’s preferences recorded.

## 4. Make and explain the decision

**Owner:** HR

Confirm authority, funding, owner and timing; explain any refusal and alternatives. Refer disputed legal questions appropriately.

**Evidence before moving on:** An accessible decision with a review/challenge route.

## 5. Implement and verify

**Owner:** process owner

Test actual work with the individual; check privacy, effort, control and timeliness.

**Evidence before moving on:** Observed effectiveness and unresolved work recorded.

## 6. Review or reopen

**Owner:** line manager

Review at the agreed time and after changes to work, manager, location, technology or needs. Reopen ineffective arrangements.

**Evidence before moving on:** Close only when support works and review/ownership continue.

## Escalation

Escalate unresolved funding or delivery to accountable leadership and HR; contested reasonableness to HR/legal; concerns about the manager through an independent route.

## Boundaries

Do not suspend urgent practical support while awaiting a diagnosis, grant or non-essential paperwork.

## Review and closure

Agree a review date, check the outcome with the affected person, and reopen ineffective arrangements. Record the outcome, remaining work, continuing support and accessible challenge route. No automatic closure on equipment purchase or form completion.
