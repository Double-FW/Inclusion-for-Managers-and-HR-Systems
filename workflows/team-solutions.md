# Explore a shared team solution

**Workflow:** W4 · **Status:** Draft operational guidance · **Coordinator:** line manager

**Trigger:** A shared process or activity creates an access barrier.

## Start with

- Person’s private preference about team involvement
- Task goal and permitted sharing
- Competing access needs and workload impacts

## 1. Agree involvement privately

**Owner:** line manager

Ask whether collaboration would help and what may be shared. Provide an individual route if team involvement is declined.

**Evidence before moving on:** Free choice and sharing boundaries.

## 2. Frame the process challenge

**Owner:** meeting organiser

Describe the goal and barrier without identifying someone’s condition.

**Evidence before moving on:** An accessible, task-centred problem statement.

## 3. Explore alternatives

**Owner:** line manager

Consider redesign and independent routes first. Optional buddying needs privacy, workload and an exit route.

**Evidence before moving on:** Options including refusal of help without penalty.

## 4. Try and test

**Owner:** process owner

Evaluate real participation with affected people, including conflicting needs; stop ineffective or exposing solutions.

**Evidence before moving on:** Evidence of access, effort, dignity and remaining issues.

## 5. Review and share process learning

**Owner:** line manager

Share anonymised learning only within agreed boundaries; keep the permanent fix owned and funded.

**Evidence before moving on:** Review date and a transferable process account, not a personal story.

## Escalation

Escalate inaccessible activity design to its owner and persistent exclusion to leadership; do not make the affected colleague responsible for entertaining or educating the team.

## Boundaries

Buddying is voluntary assistance, not an automatic substitute for independent access.

## Review and closure

Agree a review date, check the outcome with the affected person, and reopen ineffective arrangements. Record the outcome, remaining work, continuing support and accessible challenge route. No automatic closure on equipment purchase or form completion.

## Machine-use boundary

The structured file records the main sequence and conditional routes. It is human decision support, not an executable authority to decide a case. A refusal, failed trial, urgent risk or disputed decision requires the named human route; do not blindly advance to closure. `escalate` means route to the specified role while preserving support; `close` does not erase continuing arrangements or a challenge route.
