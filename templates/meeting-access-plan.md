# Meeting access plan

**Status:** Blank proposed practice template · **Owner role:** meeting organiser · **Guidance:** IE-COM-001

Use only in an approved confidential system for a real case. Keep completed cases in an approved confidential system. Accept other accessible routes and collect only information needed for the task. No diagnostic history is required by this blank form.

State why the meeting is needed, decisions sought, agenda and accessible materials.
