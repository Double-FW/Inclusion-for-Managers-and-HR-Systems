# Editorial origin

This knowledge edition was prepared for Double FW Ltd from Gareth Ford Williams’s supplied inclusive-employment ideas, the technical brief dated 4 November 2026 and the practical knowledge in the first development package. It reorganises the content for people and RAG use, preserving individual choice, barrier-focused practice and outcomes beyond compliance.

The 40 numbered suggestions and 30 unnumbered thematic units are accounted for in [the input coverage record](input-coverage.md). Practical ideas have been retained, consolidated or qualified where needed. This is an editorial synthesis, not a systematic research review, adopted employer policy or independently evaluated employment programme.

The numerical examples and worked cases are synthetic. None describe an identifiable employee or represent observed outcomes. Existing source review limits remain visible in [the source register](source-register.md).
