# Performance and formal procedures

Use when a concern is raised and before a consequential decision.

- [ ] Is the concern about a justified expectation with observable evidence?
- [ ] Have usable instructions, training, tools and delivered adjustments been checked?
- [ ] Are sustainable workload and cumulative access effort considered?
- [ ] Can the person understand the evidence, prepare and respond accessibly?
- [ ] Are barriers and disputed facts explored without diagnosing or assuming motivation?
- [ ] Are support, accompaniment, breaks and communication arrangements considered appropriately?
- [ ] Do improvement actions have owners and fair review points?
- [ ] Are urgent harassment and safety matters acted on without inappropriate delay?
- [ ] Is a consequential decision made by an authorised human with appropriate advice?
- [ ] Are reasons, correction and an independent challenge route accessible?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
