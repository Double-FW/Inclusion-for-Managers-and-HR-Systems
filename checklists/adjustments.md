# Adjustment conversation and review

Use from initial request to checking effectiveness.

- [ ] Was the request accepted through a usable route without unnecessary form or diagnostic prerequisites?
- [ ] Are the task, barrier, impact, preference and urgency understood?
- [ ] Is necessary interim support in place?
- [ ] Is practical sharing agreed and sensitive information handled privately?
- [ ] Are relevant process, technology and facilities owners involved?
- [ ] Are employer funding and any additional external funding kept distinct?
- [ ] Does the decision explain arrangement, owner, dependencies and next update?
- [ ] Have any refusal, alternatives and challenge routes been explained accessibly?
- [ ] Has the arrangement been tested in actual work, beyond the purchase or approval?
- [ ] Are changing needs, transitions, review and reopening addressed?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
