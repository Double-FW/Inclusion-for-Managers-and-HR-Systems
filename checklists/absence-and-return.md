# Absence and return to work

Use with the person to plan sustainable participation.

- [ ] Is necessary discussion private and focused on functional work implications?
- [ ] Have relevant absence-recording or trigger options been considered individually?
- [ ] Is pay and leave information explained through the appropriate HR route?
- [ ] Are proposed hours, duties, breaks and recovery coherent?
- [ ] Has workload been adjusted rather than compressed into reduced hours?
- [ ] Are equipment, training, location and access arrangements ready?
- [ ] Is safe cover or handover provided where needed?
- [ ] Are contact frequency and format agreed rather than intrusive?
- [ ] Can a short flare-up as well as a long absence trigger a useful review?
- [ ] Is there a usable route to change or challenge ineffective arrangements?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
