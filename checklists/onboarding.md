# Onboarding and transitions

Use before start and again when real work begins.

- [ ] Are building or remote access, accounts, authentication and essential tools ready?
- [ ] Have actual task paths and relevant assistive-technology arrangements been checked?
- [ ] Is the timetable, induction material and support information usable?
- [ ] Has the person been invited to discuss practical communication and work preferences privately?
- [ ] Are training, practice time, examples and a sandbox offered where useful?
- [ ] Is the optional passport focused on functional support and agreed sharing?
- [ ] Are effective arrangements preserved through manager, role or site changes?
- [ ] Is a contact outside the manager available when needed?
- [ ] Are benefits, learning and confidential support reachable from the start?
- [ ] Are early review, correction and escalation arrangements clear?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
