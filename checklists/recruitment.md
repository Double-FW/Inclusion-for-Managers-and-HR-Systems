# Recruitment and assessment

Use while designing a role and before each assessment.

- [ ] Are essential criteria tied to actual tasks rather than personality or inherited boilerplate?
- [ ] Are real working-pattern options and any necessary presence or coverage requirements explained?
- [ ] Can applicants access the advert, application, support contact and assessment information?
- [ ] Are format, criteria, expected duration, tools and preparation materials shared early?
- [ ] Have the proposed five-working-day preparation default and any justified exception been discussed as practice, not law?
- [ ] Do alternative formats measure the same relevant competence without unpaid productive work?
- [ ] Are communication support, processing time, breaks, location and remote arrangements usable?
- [ ] Are health questions targeted to a permitted purpose with appropriate HR advice?
- [ ] Are work evidence and scoring separated from personality fit, eye contact and social-media assumptions?
- [ ] Are monitoring data, retained evidence, feedback and challenge routes handled appropriately?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
