# Optional AI support

Use before and during a person-chosen trial.

- [ ] Is the task and hoped-for benefit defined by the person’s work need?
- [ ] Can the person decline, stop or change the trial without penalty?
- [ ] Does an accessible non-AI route remain available?
- [ ] Have product access, data flows, security, retention and permissions been reviewed?
- [ ] Is input synthetic or the approved minimum necessary information?
- [ ] Are drafting, sending, approving and consequential decisions kept distinct?
- [ ] Are outputs checked for omissions, errors and access?
- [ ] Is diagnosis, masking, emotion/personality inference and disability-related monitoring avoided?
- [ ] Are stop conditions, support, logging and recovery arrangements proportionate?
- [ ] Does review include effort, control, privacy and sustainability rather than novelty alone?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
