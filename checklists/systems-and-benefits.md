# Systems, benefits and procurement

Use before purchase, after change and when a defect is reported.

- [ ] Have meaningful end-to-end goals been identified, including sign-in, support and exports?
- [ ] Has the actual version/configuration been tested rather than relying on a declaration?
- [ ] Can staff use payroll, learning, benefits and confidential wellbeing services?
- [ ] Is there an interim route that preserves privacy, timeliness and dignity?
- [ ] Are defects described reproducibly with synthetic or minimum information?
- [ ] Does an internal service owner or procurement/vendor owner have responsibility?
- [ ] Is task analysis voluntary and directed at system burden rather than employee ranking?
- [ ] Are contractual remedies and payment decisions left with authorised procurement/legal roles?
- [ ] Does retesting verify the full goal and relevant interaction methods?
- [ ] Do unresolved barriers inform renewal, commissioning and permanent repair?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
