# Meetings and collaboration

Use for planning and review; ask individuals rather than prescribing from identity.

- [ ] Is the purpose clear, with an agenda and usable materials in advance?
- [ ] Can people contribute through an agreed combination of spoken, signed, written, advance or later input?
- [ ] Is AAC contribution time protected with usable turn-taking?
- [ ] Have Deaf and hard-of-hearing participants chosen relevant communication support?
- [ ] Are interpretation/caption arrangements, lighting, acoustics and sightlines workable?
- [ ] Can remote participants follow and influence the same decisions?
- [ ] Can anyone pause or ask for clarification without public disclosure or penalty?
- [ ] Are the actual tools, clients, boards and documents usable in context?
- [ ] Are recording/transcription and information-sharing arrangements appropriate?
- [ ] Are decisions, owners, dates and a realistic response window provided accessibly?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
