# Work design and environment

Use for daily work, location and schedule changes.

- [ ] Have essential results been separated from habitual methods?
- [ ] Are work patterns assessed against actual tasks, safety and coverage?
- [ ] Are priorities, milestones and changed expectations clear and findable?
- [ ] Is workload sustainable given access effort, working time and recovery?
- [ ] Have desks, acoustics, lighting, routes, toilets and relevant equipment been considered with the person?
- [ ] Have conflicting needs such as quiet and voice input been explored without ranking identities?
- [ ] Are alarms, emergency arrangements and safe handover considered when choosing support?
- [ ] Is a discreet pause/recovery route agreed in advance?
- [ ] Is notice and accessible training provided before change wherever possible?
- [ ] Can arrangements be revisited without repeatedly proving a diagnosis?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
