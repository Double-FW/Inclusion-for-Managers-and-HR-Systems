# Shared activities and team solutions

Use before collaborative problem-solving, events or optional social activity.

- [ ] Does the affected person want team involvement, and what may be shared?
- [ ] Is the problem framed around the activity or process rather than a colleague’s identity?
- [ ] Is redesign or an equally valued accessible activity considered?
- [ ] Is assistance optional, with a route to independent participation?
- [ ] Have privacy, status, workload and competing needs been considered?
- [ ] Can someone decline without loss of opportunity or pressure to explain health?
- [ ] Does sign-language learning or awareness activity remain optional and avoid replacing professional access support?
- [ ] Has the proposed solution been tried in context?
- [ ] Are the permanent fix and review owned by someone with authority?
- [ ] Is shared learning non-identifying and within agreed permissions?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
