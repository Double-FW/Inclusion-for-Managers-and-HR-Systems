# Culture, development and progression

Use when reviewing opportunities, learning and reward.

- [ ] Do published criteria reflect actual work rather than personality conformity?
- [ ] Can flexible, part-time, remote and shift staff access learning and influential work?
- [ ] Are mentoring and networks optional, accessible and resourced?
- [ ] Are specialist/technical routes considered alongside people management where supported?
- [ ] Are aspirations discussed without diagnostic assumptions or protective steering?
- [ ] Does training include practical disability, Deaf and neurodivergence awareness without singling out staff?
- [ ] Is lived-experience contribution accessible, chosen and appropriately compensated?
- [ ] Are team activities designed for meaningful participation rather than compulsory assistance?
- [ ] Are redundancy, redeployment, exit and reward processes included in review?
- [ ] Do measures consider experience and opportunity rather than training totals or scheme status alone?

## After the discussion

What remains unresolved? Who can act, what interim access is available, when is the next update, and how can the person request review? A completed checklist is not evidence that access worked.
