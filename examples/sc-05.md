# Reduced distraction without a diagnosis

**Synthetic case:** SC-05 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

An employee asks for a less distracting desk while awaiting an assessment.

## Example response

Discuss the task and helpful environment, try practical support and name a review owner without waiting for a diagnostic label.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: A person can raise a barrier and receive a clear next step. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not diagnose or state that legal disability has been proven.

## Verification and unresolved work

Ask whether concentration, task completion and sustainable effort improved. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
