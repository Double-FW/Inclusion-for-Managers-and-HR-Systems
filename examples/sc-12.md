# AI support is ineffective or declined

**Synthetic case:** SC-12 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A summarising tool omits key decisions; the participant wants to stop.

## Example response

Stop the trial, restore the accessible non-AI route, record the failure without penalty and review options with the person.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: People can trial useful support with choice, privacy and a workable alternative. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not make AI use compulsory or send personnel information to an unapproved service.

## Verification and unresolved work

Verify retained information, correction and recovery; do not claim observed benefit. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
