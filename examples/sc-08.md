# An adjustment through manager change

**Synthetic case:** SC-08 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A new manager tells a staff member to reapply for every agreed arrangement.

## Example response

Plan a private handover with the person and preserve effective support while checking the new work context.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Agreed support survives transitions without unnecessary repeated disclosure. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not share the whole medical history or automatically withdraw arrangements.

## Verification and unresolved work

Confirm agreed owners and that access continued during transition. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
