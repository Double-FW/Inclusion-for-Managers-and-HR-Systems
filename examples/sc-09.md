# Performance during a software failure

**Synthetic case:** SC-09 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A colleague misses a deadline because required forms cannot be operated by keyboard.

## Example response

Check the defect, delivered support and actual workload; fix access and agree fair review with HR where needed.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Concerns are assessed on fair evidence with access and support in place. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not rank the worker against a mouse user or assume every performance concern is disability-related.

## Verification and unresolved work

Review actual work evidence after support and retain the independent challenge route. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
