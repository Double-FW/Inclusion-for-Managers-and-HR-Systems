# An inaccessible wellbeing benefit

**Synthetic case:** SC-04 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A screen-reader user cannot complete authentication for a mental-health support benefit.

## Example response

The HR commissioning owner and service owner arrange confidential direct access while procurement routes the defect.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: People can use essential services with confidentiality and timely access. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not require a colleague to read private wellbeing information or declare automatic discrimination.

## Verification and unresolved work

Retest authentication and the full service using synthetic data and the relevant interaction method. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
