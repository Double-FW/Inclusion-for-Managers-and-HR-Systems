# Return with fluctuating capacity

**Synthetic case:** SC-11 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A phased return has reduced hours but unchanged deliverables and automatic absence triggers.

## Example response

Discuss sustainable workload, support, individual recording/trigger options, pay information and reviews through HR.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Attendance arrangements and return plans account for individual circumstances. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not promise all absence is exempt or compress full workload into reduced hours.

## Verification and unresolved work

Check the real work and support rather than attendance alone. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
