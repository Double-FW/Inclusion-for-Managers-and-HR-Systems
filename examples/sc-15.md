# An inaccessible team day

**Synthetic case:** SC-15 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

An activity requires independent climbing and the organiser offers compulsory buddying.

## Example response

Discuss privately, redesign or replace the activity, and offer voluntary assistance alongside equally valued independent participation.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Teams can improve a process while respecting choice and independence. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not turn the person’s exclusion into a puzzle or publish their story without permission.

## Verification and unresolved work

Evaluate choice, status, privacy and all participants’ access. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
