# A personality-screening proposal

**Synthetic case:** SC-10 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A hiring team wants to reject applicants with a low culture-fit personality type.

## Example response

Apply the proposed policy against typology-based selection; use a structured relevant work rubric.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Selection does not rely on personality conformity or covert profiling. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not claim that all psychometric methods are scientifically invalid or secretly retain fun-test profiles.

## Verification and unresolved work

Review the criteria and actual evidence; an employer must adopt the policy before it is treated as local authority. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
