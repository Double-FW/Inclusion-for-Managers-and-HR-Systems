# A supplier-payment question

**Synthetic case:** SC-14 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A team manager proposes refusing payment because a supplier claimed accessibility.

## Example response

Preserve defect and contract evidence, maintain staff access and refer remedy/payment decisions to procurement/legal.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Supplier and internal commitments lead to verified fixes. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not threaten payment refusal or declare breach established.

## Verification and unresolved work

Track agreed repair, verification and unresolved contract decisions separately. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
