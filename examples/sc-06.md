# Flexibility for a shift worker

**Synthetic case:** SC-06 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A manager says nurses cannot have flexibility because the job is on site.

## Example response

Examine actual duties, rota, cover, breaks and options; HR identifies adjustment and flexible-working routes.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Working arrangements support sustainable contribution and real job needs. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not promise remote work or exclude the whole occupation.

## Verification and unresolved work

Review the person’s needs alongside safe cover and sustainable workload. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
