# Small-group reporting

**Synthetic case:** SC-16 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A dashboard would expose two employees’ health categories and promotion outcomes.

## Example response

Minimise collection, involve privacy expertise, aggregate/suppress appropriately and separate support from monitoring.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Learning does not create intrusive employment or disability profiles. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not invent a universal anonymity threshold or infer disability from tool use.

## Verification and unresolved work

Review reidentification risk and usefulness before any data collection or reporting. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
