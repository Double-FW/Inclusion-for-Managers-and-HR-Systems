# A signer needs interpretation

**Synthetic case:** SC-03 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A complex planning meeting offers only automatic captions to a participant who needs signed communication.

## Example response

Book appropriate qualified interpretation, plan sightlines/content access and test the setup with the participant.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Participants can follow the full discussion and respond. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Team sign-language learning or short written exchanges do not substitute for full communication access.

## Verification and unresolved work

Verify access to contributions, presentation and decisions, not merely interpreter attendance. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
