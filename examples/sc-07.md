# Quiet work and voice input

**Synthetic case:** SC-07 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

One colleague needs quiet while another relies on voice input in a shared area.

## Example response

Ask both privately, consider rooms, acoustic changes and scheduling, and trial a workable combination.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: People can use the workplace safely and comfortably enough to do their work. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not rank diagnoses, remove someone’s necessary input method or make quiet space physically inaccessible.

## Verification and unresolved work

Check both goals and workloads after the trial. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
