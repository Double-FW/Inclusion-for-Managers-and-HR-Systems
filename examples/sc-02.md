# A Deaf colleague who does not sign

**Synthetic case:** SC-02 · **Evaluation status:** Not run with users or an assistant.

## Situation

A manager books a sign interpreter without asking a Deaf colleague who relies on captions and text.

## Example response

Ask privately, arrange the requested usable support and test captions, acoustics and turn-taking.

## Why this fits the guidance

The response aims for this outcome: Participants can follow the full discussion and respond. It assigns the relevant owner and leaves any rights-sensitive decision with authorised people. Interim access should continue while outstanding work is resolved.

## Boundary

Do not prescribe signing from Deaf identity.

## Verification and unresolved work

Check the full conversation and response route with the colleague, including remote participants. The action owner should agree a next update, an accessible review route and how unresolved work will be tracked. No real outcome, legal approval or user satisfaction result is claimed by this illustration.
