# Roles in inclusive employment

These reading routes share the same core guidance. Inclusion is a working relationship between people and the owners of the systems, processes and environments they use; it should not become the responsibility of the person experiencing a barrier.

| Role | Primary contribution | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hiring managers and recruiters | Relevant role requirements and accessible assessment | [Hiring](hiring-managers.md) |
| HR and people professionals | Fair procedures, support coordination and confidential records | [HR](hr.md) |
| Line managers | Practical conversations, work design and follow-through | [Line managers](line-managers.md) |
| Colleagues and teams | Accessible collaboration and respectful shared problem-solving | [Teams](teams.md) |
| Employees and applicants | Describe barriers, shape support and challenge decisions | [Employees](employees.md) |
| Leaders and service owners | Resources, accountable delivery and systemic repair | [Leadership and owners](leaders-and-service-owners.md) |

The person affected should influence the response without having to disclose unnecessarily, become an expert in internal ownership, or fix the organisation's barriers alone. See [responsibilities and escalation](../processes/responsibilities-and-escalation.md).
