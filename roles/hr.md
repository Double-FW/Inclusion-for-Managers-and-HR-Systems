# HR and people professionals

Own fair procedures, confidential support records and routes to review disputed decisions. This is a draft guidance route, not your employer's adopted policy. The same canonical guidance is used by every role.

1. Start with [the relevant guidance](../docs/06-adjustments.md) and [a practical template](../templates/adjustment-decision.md).
2. For a support need, use [adjustments](../workflows/adjustments.md): ask privately, provide interim access, name an owner and next update, and review effectiveness.
3. For a tool or benefit failure, use [system barriers](../workflows/system-barriers.md). Service owners, procurement or IT must be involved; staff should not have to navigate every boundary themselves.
4. For a performance or attendance concern, use [fair review](../workflows/fair-performance.md) before attributing a failure to the person.
5. For shared improvement, use [team solutions](../workflows/team-solutions.md). For technology experiments, use [AI trials](../workflows/ai-trials.md).

## Escalation and privacy

Use the employer's confidential HR, employee-relations, union or independent reporting route if the manager is involved, the issue is disputed or urgent access is unresolved. Ask for the contact, next step and expected update date. Seek appropriate advice on employment rights and deadlines; this guide does not calculate claims or limitation dates.

Use a confidential organisational route for a real case. Ask what information is needed, why, who will see it and how to correct it. A manager should explain an adverse decision and its review route in an accessible format. Urgent safety or harassment concerns require prompt action through the relevant organisational route.

## What to check after action

Can the person achieve the goal, with acceptable effort, independence, privacy and timeliness? Has an owner accepted unresolved work? Is a review date agreed? Training, equipment approval or a completed form alone does not settle these questions.
