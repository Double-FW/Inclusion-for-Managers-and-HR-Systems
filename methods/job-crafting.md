# Job crafting and sustainable work design

## Identify the essential result

Describe what the role needs to achieve, the necessary quality and any genuine coverage, safety or coordination constraints. Separate these from habits such as everyone starting at the same time, all decisions happening aloud or all progression requiring people management.

## Explore the design with the person

Consider task sequence, work location, breaks, focus time, communication, responsibility mix and access to training. Job crafting can align the work with a person's interests and methods, but it should not stereotype a diagnostic group or quietly narrow a career. A person may want development in a less familiar task rather than permanent removal from it.

Check the whole team and service. Redistribution needs time, resources and recognition; otherwise a well-intended arrangement can create pressure or resentment. Share the practical plan without unnecessary health disclosure.

## Trial and review

Agree the goal, proposed change, owner, duration, support and evidence of a workable outcome. Examine quality, sustainable effort, autonomy, coverage and development opportunity. If output improves only through excessive unpaid work or recovery, the arrangement is not sustainable. Review changing needs and context rather than treating a trial as an irreversible contract with the employee.
