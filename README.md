# Inclusive Employment — Knowledge Edition

**Double FW Ltd · Edition 1.0 · 4 November 2026**

A Markdown knowledge collection for people and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). It explains barriers, practical approaches, methodologies, working arrangements, opportunities and outcomes across the employment lifecycle. The aim is equitable participation, dignity, autonomy and satisfaction, with legal compliance as an essential baseline rather than the whole objective.

## How to interpret the knowledge

The guidance describes recommended practice and options for local consideration; it is not automatically an employer's adopted policy. Legal material provides questions and source routes, with Great Britain and Northern Ireland kept distinct. It does not determine a person's rights, liability, funding award or dispute. The sources retain the review dates and limitations recorded in the original package; this edition is a reorganisation rather than a new legal audit.

Each main guidance section has a stable ID, intended outcome, responsible role, explanation, boundary and source references. For retrieval, keep those elements together: a useful option can become misleading when separated from its context or qualification. Scenario outcomes are illustrative, and the numerical example datasets are explicitly synthetic. No live employment case or research result is implied.

Use the source material selectively for the question at hand. A diagnosis, template, checklist score or fastest task time cannot substitute for understanding the individual and the work. No software implementation is required to read or use these documents.

## Privacy and choice

This collection contains guidance, blank templates and fictional examples. Keep actual health, applicant, passport, performance and grievance records in approved confidential systems. Involve affected people, offer usable ways to contribute and respect decisions about disclosure, assistance and experimentation. See [privacy and records](guidance/privacy-and-records.md).
