# Acceptance criteria

Technical checks support a review package. They cannot mark unperformed human or legal evaluation passed.

| Gate | Evidence required | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| scope_traceability | Forty numbered and thirty thematic input units, eleven modules and five workflows. | technical_check |
| practical_utility | Role users can find effective actions and challenge routes. | pending_human_review |
| editorial_integrity | No diagnostic prescription, unsupported certainty or hidden policy adoption. | pending_human_review |
| legal_integrity | Current GB law, status coverage, commencement and NI boundaries reviewed. | pending_qualified_review |
| operational_completeness | Owners, interim access, evidence, review and challenge routes present and usable. | technical_check_plus_human_review |
| technical_quality | Schemas, IDs, links, anchors, workflow order and regression tests. | technical_check |
| accessibility | Representative rendered content reviewed with keyboard, zoom/reflow and screen reader. | pending_manual_review |
| human_evaluation | Accessible compensated evaluation with disabled, Deaf and neurodivergent people and role users. | not_run |
| privacy_ai | No real case data; local template, metrics, service and AI approval still needed. | pending_adoption_review |
| dependencies_rights | Five dependencies unavailable; upstream reuse and owner distribution licence unresolved. | pending_owner_decision |

## Release decision

The release owner records which scope is approved, who reviewed it, applicable revisions, dates and limitations. A technical pass is not public-release approval. Legal authority, local policy adoption, rights and confidential reporting arrangements must not be inferred. Scenarios are expected outcomes until actually run; preserve failures and unresolved limitations.
