# Role design and recruitment

## Specify essential outcomes

<a id="role-outcomes"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-REC-001 · **Action owner:** hiring manager

**Intended outcome:** Candidates are assessed against work that is genuinely required.

Begin with the purpose of the role, its essential tasks and the evidence that would show someone can perform them with appropriate support. Replace inherited requirements such as “dynamic multitasker”, “confident personality” and “excellent communicator” with the actual task, audience and outcome. A tester might need to document a reproducible fault; that does not automatically require confident public speaking. Do not assume technical jobs have no communication duties.

Describe real location, safety, coverage and collaboration needs and which working patterns can vary. Examine shifts, start/finish times, job shares, remote activities and task allocation even for predominantly on-site work. Do not promise hybrid work that the employer cannot provide or exclude an occupation wholesale.

Publish pay, assessment format, expected duration, tools and an accessible adjustment contact. Review whether qualifications, uninterrupted employment and particular institutions are necessary rather than proxies for capability. Separate equality monitoring from the selection panel.

**Boundary:** Anonymisation can reduce some cues; it does not remove bias from criteria or scoring.

## Prepare and assess fairly

<a id="assessment"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-REC-002 · **Action owner:** hiring manager

**Intended outcome:** Applicants can demonstrate relevant competence through accessible assessment.

Give candidates the criteria, format, practical access details and preparation materials early. As a proposed local default, share interview questions and tasks at least five working days ahead. This is not a statutory period. If unrehearsed performance is essential, document why and design a fair accessible assessment; do not turn test security into a blanket refusal of adjustments.

Offer structured interviews, work samples or paid trials according to the competence being assessed. Avoid unpaid productive work and seek HR advice on trial payment and status. Use the same explicit rubric and equivalent evidence standard across alternative routes. Do not reward eye contact, smiling, fast banter, accent or social similarity. Allow processing time and the approved tools and communication support.

Check the room, quiet, temperature, lighting, routes and remote connection. Supply written directions and, where helpful, images with text descriptions. Invite access requests without asking unnecessary health questions; consult the legal page before pre-offer enquiries.

**Boundary:** Five days and paid-trial design are proposed practice; section 60 health-question restrictions have separate legal authority.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [L6](../evidence/source-register.md#l6).

## Selection boundaries and evidence

<a id="selection-boundaries"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-REC-003 · **Action owner:** hr

**Intended outcome:** Selection does not rely on personality conformity or covert profiling.

Proposed policy: do not use personality typologies, personality-fit scores or unsupported trait inferences for selection, performance, task opportunity or promotion. Prefer role-relevant evidence. Do not repeat the claim that every psychometric method is fake science: instrument quality and intended use need specific evaluation. Any proposed cognitive or aptitude test needs documented necessity, validity, accessibility, professional review, adjustment arrangements and a challenge route.

Do not conduct informal screening of candidates' personal social-media accounts. Where lawful role-specific vetting is proposed, require transparent, proportionate, reviewed criteria rather than discretionary searches. Do not infer health, religion or suitability from incidental material.

Optional personality activities can still cause harm. If used socially, participation must be consequence-free; do not retain profiles, label colleagues or quietly use results in future decisions. Do not use AI emotion, facial-expression, personality or disability inference. Tell candidates how relevant assessment evidence is used, retained and challenged.

**Boundary:** These selection restrictions are proposed repository policy, not a claim that every excluded activity is inherently unlawful.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief).
