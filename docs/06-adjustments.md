# Adjustments and funding

**Status:** Draft practice guidance, version 0.1.0. Organisational practices are proposed until adopted. Legal summaries are separately marked and require qualified review.

## Open a practical support conversation

<a id="conversation"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-ADJ-001 · **Action owner:** line manager

**Intended outcome:** A person can raise a barrier and receive a clear next step.

Accept requests through speech, text or another usable route. A person does not need to use the legal phrase “reasonable adjustment” to start a practical discussion. Ask privately what task is affected, the impact, what helps, what has been tried and what they want to happen next. Do not demand a diagnosis for ordinary support.

Agree what information needs sharing and with whom. Ask whether the current difficulty creates an urgent access, safety, pay or confidentiality issue, and provide interim support promptly. Name the coordinator and date of the next update. If relevant functional advice is needed, explain why and request only proportionate information, using HR or occupational health where helpful.

Use the adjustment workflow and templates to record decisions rather than relying on a conversation that disappears. Keep case information in the approved confidential system. Provide another contact where trust in the manager is low or the manager cannot resolve the issue.

**Boundary:** Practical support need not await diagnosis; legal disability and reasonableness questions still require context and appropriate advice.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [L1](../evidence/source-register.md#l1), [L11](../evidence/source-register.md#l11).

## Decide, implement and review

<a id="decide-review"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-ADJ-002 · **Action owner:** hr

**Intended outcome:** An effective adjustment has ownership, implementation and a fair challenge route.

Consider changes to methods, arrangements, tools, environment and support with the person. Involve the owners who can deliver each part. Record the barrier, proposed response, implementation owner, funding, dependencies, interim support and review date. A purchased licence or approved request is not proof of working access.

Test the arrangement in actual work and ask about effort, independence, privacy and sustainability. Reopen the case if it is ineffective or needs change. Review after a change to tasks, manager, location, technology or needs. Do not turn routine review into a recurring demand to prove disability or remove working support without considering consequences.

Where a request cannot be agreed, give an accessible explanation, identify alternatives, retain interim access where possible and explain how to seek a review outside the original decision-maker. Escalate disputed legal reasonableness to HR/legal. Adopt local response targets explicitly; do not invent statutory adjustment deadlines.

**Boundary:** Decisions are individual; neither a catalogue nor a previous employee case settles what is reasonable in another case.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [L1](../evidence/source-register.md#l1).

## Separate employer funding from additional support

<a id="funding"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-ADJ-003 · **Action owner:** hr

**Intended outcome:** Funding processes do not transfer the burden to the employee or delay access.

The employer is responsible for funding adjustments it is legally required to make. Coordinate the appropriate internal budget and escalation rather than making an employee negotiate departmental accounts. Access to Work may support eligible additional needs; it is not a substitute for employer-funded reasonable adjustments. Check current eligibility and agreed costs directly with the scheme.

Provide help with the application where the person wants it, but do not promise a grant, an amount or a reimbursement date. Keep employer action moving while external decisions are pending. Record who will pay, order and maintain equipment and who resolves gaps. Funding information and service availability can change, so recheck before giving specific advice.

Offer relevant coaching, support workers or technology according to the person and task rather than assuming these are necessary for a diagnostic group. Follow the separate Northern Ireland route where applicable; do not silently apply the GB scheme to another jurisdiction.

**Boundary:** Grant eligibility, contribution and reimbursement are scheme decisions; this guide makes no funding guarantee.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [L3](../evidence/source-register.md#l3).
