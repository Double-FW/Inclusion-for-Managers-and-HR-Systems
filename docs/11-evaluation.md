# Evaluation and improvement

**Status:** Draft practice guidance, version 0.1.0. Organisational practices are proposed until adopted. Legal summaries are separately marked and require qualified review.

## Measure the experience and the response

<a id="outcomes"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-EVA-001 · **Action owner:** knowledge base owner

**Intended outcome:** Improvement reflects actual participation rather than activity counts.

Track adjustment acknowledgement and implementation, whether support works for the person, recurring barriers and unresolved access to essential services. Include independence, effort, errors, fatigue, confidence, satisfaction, belonging, learning and progression. Training completion, scheme membership and equipment purchases describe activity; they do not prove outcomes.

Define each measure's purpose, denominator, time window, missing data and limits before collecting it. Review early retention at relevant intervals, including 12–24 months where useful, but do not infer causation from a departure or assume retention proves inclusion. Lower complaint counts can mean fear or inaccessible reporting; include confidence in raising concerns.

Combine operational evidence with voluntary accessible feedback and appropriately compensated lived-experience participation. Examine recurring process failures and cumulative burden. Give leaders the decisions and resources required, not a league table of people who disclose needs. Validate whether a repair remains effective after change.

**Boundary:** Small samples and self-selection limit conclusions; avoid unsupported causal claims or universal timing tolerances.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [DFW-10](../evidence/source-register.md#dfw-10), [DFW-11](../evidence/source-register.md#dfw-11).

## Collect less and protect meaning

<a id="data-ethics"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-EVA-002 · **Action owner:** privacy owner

**Intended outcome:** Learning does not create intrusive employment or disability profiles.

Separate confidential support records from optional equality monitoring. Determine lawful purpose, necessary data, access controls, retention and relevant health-data conditions before collection. Agreement about a meeting preference does not automatically authorise all health-data processing. Do not infer disability from use of assistive technology or a particular tool.

Use aggregated insights where possible and review small-group suppression and reporting granularity with privacy expertise. Do not publish a universal group-size threshold as law. Participation should not affect opportunities, performance or access to support. Provide accessible information and routes to correct inaccuracies and challenge consequential decisions.

Adapt customer-data methods carefully to the power relationships of employment. Avoid diagnosis-level segmentation without justified need. Never put completed templates, raw research, health records or identifiable grievances in this repository, even if the repository is private. Use approved confidential systems for actual cases.

**Boundary:** The knowledge base provides synthetic structures, not authority or infrastructure to process employee cases.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [DFW-07](../evidence/source-register.md#dfw-07), [L7](../evidence/source-register.md#l7).

## Connect commitments to ownership and review

<a id="governance"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-EVA-003 · **Action owner:** executive sponsor

**Intended outcome:** Commitments result in funded action and honest public claims.

Assign an executive sponsor, operational owner and responsible roles for HR, IT, procurement, facilities, privacy and review. Connect commitments to activities, resources, evidence and reporting. Champions enable local capability with protected time and support; they do not absorb every unresolved case.

Review modules annually as proposed local policy, with quarterly checks of legal/funding dependencies and AI guidance and immediate triage after material change or harm. Do not adopt these intervals as legal deadlines. Assess upstream changes before merging them; preserve local qualifications and approval status.

Record conflicting evidence, failed trials and unavailable sources. A maturity method is not certification, and a voluntary scheme is not proof of legal compliance. Keep claims proportionate to evidence. Include disabled, Deaf and neurodivergent people in setting priorities and evaluating results, without forcing representatives to speak for every individual.

**Boundary:** Owners, budgets and approvals must be appointed by the organisation; this draft does not invent them.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [DFW-11](../evidence/source-register.md#dfw-11), [DFW-12](../evidence/source-register.md#dfw-12), [DFW-16](../evidence/source-register.md#dfw-16), [DFW-17](../evidence/source-register.md#dfw-17).
