## Evaluate access before attributing poor performance

<a id="fair-review"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-PER-001 · **Action owner:** line manager

**Intended outcome:** Concerns are assessed on fair evidence with access and support in place.

State the work outcome, justified expectation and observable issue. Check whether the instructions, training, workload, tools and agreed adjustments were usable and delivered. Ask privately about barriers and allow the person to respond in a usable format. Separate observed facts from assumptions about motivation, personality or health.

Correct access failures and agree a sustainable plan with specific actions, support, owners and review points. Do not rate eye contact, smiling, speaking speed or participation in optional social activities as competence. Consider work quality and actual role outcomes without ignoring cumulative access effort or necessary recovery.

Investigating barriers does not mean every concern is disability-related or prevent fair action on misconduct. Do not delay action on harassment or immediate safety risks while exploring adjustments. Bring HR into serious or disputed cases and provide an independent challenge route. Keep confidential case records outside this repository.

**Boundary:** This process supports fair judgement and does not determine whether a particular disciplinary decision is lawful.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief).

## Absence, recovery and returning to work

<a id="absence-return"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-PER-002 · **Action owner:** hr

**Intended outcome:** Attendance arrangements and return plans account for individual circumstances.

Discuss what absence or medical appointments mean for the work, with appropriate privacy. Consider adjusted trigger points, separate recording or disability leave where relevant, rather than mechanically applying a standard trigger. Agree the approach and explain how records will be used; do not promise that all disability-related absence is automatically excluded.

Plan return with the person, including phased hours or duties, usable equipment, updated training, breaks, coverage and review dates. Check workload rather than compressing a full workload into reduced hours. Explain pay and leave implications through the appropriate HR route, without making unsupported entitlement promises.

Changes may be needed after a short flare-up as well as a long absence. Keep communication predictable and proportionate while the person is away. Before redundancy, redeployment or other consequential decisions, seek appropriate HR/legal review of access, criteria and adjustments rather than treating absence data as a neutral score.

**Boundary:** Absence adjustments are context-specific; the guide does not prescribe unlimited leave, pay or an automatic exemption from review.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [L5](../evidence/source-register.md#l5).

## Make formal procedures accessible

<a id="formal-process"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-PER-003 · **Action owner:** hr

**Intended outcome:** People can understand, participate in and challenge formal decisions.

Provide clear notice of the issue, relevant material, preparation time and accessible response methods. Agree formats, communication support, breaks and meeting arrangements. Explain the applicable accompaniment rights and consider whether another support person is needed as an adjustment or permitted by policy; these are distinct grounds.

A statutory companion category is not necessarily the limit of support that may be appropriate. Equally, do not promise an unrestricted legal right to any chosen advocate in every meeting. Obtain advice where rights, confidentiality, representation or conflicting roles are disputed. Ensure the person can review the record and raise corrections.

Give an accessible decision with reasons and a route to appeal or independent review. Offer a safe reporting route for bullying, harassment, victimisation or a manager's conduct. Explain limits on confidentiality and avoid retaliation. Urgent safety or safeguarding action should continue through appropriate channels.

**Boundary:** Detailed procedural entitlements depend on meeting type, status and jurisdiction; verify current law and policy.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../evidence/source-register.md#brief), [L12](../evidence/source-register.md#l12).
