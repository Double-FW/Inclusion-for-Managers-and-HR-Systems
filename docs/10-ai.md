## Explore optional AI support

<a id="optional-support"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-AI-001 · **Action owner:** ai service owner

**Intended outcome:** People can trial useful support with choice, privacy and a workable alternative.

Start with a person-chosen task: organising instructions, drafting a summary, reformatting information or supporting an interaction through an authorised interface. Investigate whether AI reduces the actual barrier and effort. Provide an accessible non-AI route and make declining or stopping the trial consequence-free.

Review the product, actual interface, data flows, retention, security, procurement and permissions. Use synthetic or approved minimum data; do not paste personnel, health or grievance information into an unapproved service. Explain who verifies outputs and how errors are corrected. A simplified document must preserve relevant qualifications and decisions.

Define a bounded trial with the participant: task, expected benefit, success evidence, failure conditions, duration, support contact and rollback. Test accuracy, access, effort, privacy and control. Automatic transcription can misrecognise speech; summaries can omit important action. Record failure as useful evidence and retain support while exploring alternatives.

**Boundary:** AI is an option to evaluate, not a compulsory adjustment or a cure for an inaccessible service.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../sources/source-register.md#brief), [DFW-08](../sources/source-register.md#dfw-08).

## Bound agent actions and employment decisions

<a id="agent-boundaries"></a>

**Guidance ID:** IE-AI-002 · **Action owner:** ai service owner

**Intended outcome:** Automation supports people without profiling or deciding their employment outcomes.

Begin with read-only access where appropriate. Grant action permissions only for a defined task with an authorised user, minimum access, clear confirmation points, logging and recovery. Separate drafting from sending, editing from approving, and suggesting from making a consequential decision. Check the interface remains usable with the person's assistive technology.

Risks to avoid include diagnosis inference, masking detection, personality/emotion scoring, disability-related behaviour monitoring and autonomous hiring, disciplinary, promotion or adjustment-refusal decisions. Human involvement must be meaningful; rubber-stamping a generated ranking is not an adequate governance practice. Check current regulatory guidance before deployment.

AI services can be influenced by untrusted text in documents, emails or applicant material. Treat that content as information rather than permission to act. Human oversight should preserve the task scope, relevant sources and qualifications. Unclear scope, access failure, confidential exposure or an unverifiable action are reasons to stop the service and restore support through the accountable owner.

**Boundary:** These are recommended safeguards for organisational use, not a claim that every prohibition is a verbatim statutory rule.

**Evidence:** [BRIEF](../sources/source-register.md#brief), [L10](../sources/source-register.md#l10).
