# Barrier catalogue

A non-exhaustive set of barrier patterns drawn from the supplied ideas and related guidance. These are contextual possibilities, not diagnoses or findings about a population. A suggested approach needs individual discussion and outcome review.

| ID | Context | Barrier pattern | Approach to explore | Intended outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B01 | Attraction | Vague personality requirements | Define the actual task, audience and outcome | Fair opportunity to apply |
| B02 | Attraction | Unexplained presence requirements | Describe essential duties and genuine flexibility | Informed choice about the role |
| B03 | Application | Inaccessible forms or authentication | Offer a usable route and repair the system | Independent application |
| B04 | Assessment | Surprise format or questions | Provide preparation and justify essential unrehearsed elements | Relevant competence becomes visible |
| B05 | Assessment | Rapid conversational pressure | Offer processing time and equivalent work evidence | Fair response opportunity |
| B06 | Assessment | Personality-fit scoring | Use explicit role-relevant criteria | Selection based on work evidence |
| B07 | Assessment | Covert personal-account screening | Use transparent proportionate reviewed vetting where genuinely needed | Less arbitrary inference |
| B08 | Assessment | Directions available only as images | Add clear text routes and contact information | Usable arrival or connection |
| B09 | Onboarding | Essential tools unavailable at start | Confirm provision and actual task use before arrival | A workable first day |
| B10 | Onboarding | Single fast training session | Offer usable practice, examples and paced formats | Learning without avoidable overload |
| B11 | Communication | AAC contributions interrupted | Protect completion time and turn-taking | Influence in discussion |
| B12 | Communication | Speech is the only route | Agree complementary communication modes | Participation and choice |
| B13 | Communication | Signing assumed from Deaf identity | Ask language and access preferences | Relevant support |
| B14 | Communication | Caption errors or poor acoustics | Test quality and arrange appropriate support | Understanding the full conversation |
| B15 | Communication | Unclear agenda or quick-chat invitation | Explain purpose, preparation and decisions | Predictability and confidence |
| B16 | Communication | Verbal changes are hard to retrieve | Provide an accessible durable summary | Recognition rather than memory reliance |
| B17 | Communication | Immediate responses required | Allow realistic processing and later contribution | Considered participation |
| B18 | Collaboration | Inaccessible digital whiteboard | Provide a usable contribution route and escalate repair | Shared influence and information |
| B19 | Documents | Unreadable PDF or chart-only information | Use structured text and equivalent data/context | Informational access |
| B20 | Work patterns | Rigid hours unrelated to outcomes | Explore actual duties, cover and flexible options | Sustainable contribution |
| B21 | Workload | Output ignores access effort | Review cumulative burden and realistic allocation | Fair sustainable expectations |
| B22 | Environment | Hot-desking disrupts usable setup | Consider predictable location and equipment | Stable access |
| B23 | Environment | Noise, glare or other sensory barriers | Explore chosen environmental changes | Comfort and sustainable focus |
| B24 | Environment | Quiet provision conflicts with voice input | Explore space, acoustic and scheduling combinations | Access for both needs |
| B25 | Environment | Recovery requires public explanation | Agree a discreet pause and safe cover | Dignity and safety |
| B26 | Adjustments | Diagnosis or form delays ordinary support | Begin a practical conversation and interim access | Timely support |
| B27 | Adjustments | No owner or next update | Name coordinator and delivery responsibility | A clear route forward |
| B28 | Adjustments | Support is purchased but ineffective | Test actual work and reopen the arrangement | Effective assistance |
| B29 | Transitions | Manager change removes support | Plan agreed confidential continuity | Less repeated negotiation |
| B30 | Funding | Grant application stalls employer action | Separate internal duties and additional scheme support | Access while funding is explored |
| B31 | Benefits | Confidential wellbeing access is blocked | Arrange a private effective service route | Equal access to support |
| B32 | Systems | Keyboard or AT task path fails | Use task-based repair and full-goal retesting | Independent completion |
| B33 | Procurement | Declaration accepted without evidence | Test real tasks, version and support route | Reliable commissioning |
| B34 | Performance | Barrier treated as poor motivation | Check tools, expectations and delivered support | Fair evidence and judgement |
| B35 | Absence | Standard trigger ignores circumstances | Discuss relevant individual arrangements with HR | Fair review |
| B36 | Return | Full workload compressed into reduced hours | Match task load to the return plan | Sustainable return |
| B37 | Progression | Social visibility substitutes for contribution | Publish relevant evidence and review opportunity allocation | Fair development and reward |
| B38 | Team activity | Compulsory buddying replaces redesign | Offer chosen assistance and meaningful accessible participation | Status, choice and independence |
| B39 | AI | Support becomes compulsory or unverified | Use optional bounded trials with alternatives and recovery | Control and useful assistance |
| B40 | Evaluation | Disclosure and small-group monitoring expose staff | Minimise data and review aggregation/privacy | Learning without intrusive profiling |
