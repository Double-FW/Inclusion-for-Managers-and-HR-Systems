# Privacy and safe reporting

This repository contains guidance, blank forms and synthetic cases. Do not commit real health, passport, payroll, applicant, grievance or identifiable research information, including to a private repository. Use approved confidential systems with appropriate access, lawful purpose, retention and correction routes. Employee agreement about practical sharing does not automatically establish valid consent for health-data use.

Do not disclose a real case in an issue or pull request. For an organisational concern use the employer's confidential HR, security, privacy or independent reporting route. No reporting address has been invented; the deploying owner must publish and maintain an appropriate private contact before operational use.

If sensitive information is posted: stop further sharing; restrict access where authorised; contact the relevant privacy/security owner through the established private route; preserve only necessary incident evidence privately. Deleting the visible text may not remove Git history, caches or copies. Owners must assess further containment and any legal notification duties; this document does not declare a breach or calculate a notification deadline.

AI services need approval for data, retention, permissions and use. Do not paste case material into an unapproved service or treat an assistive-technology session as permission to record or train. CI must run against repository guidance only and must not process employee cases.
