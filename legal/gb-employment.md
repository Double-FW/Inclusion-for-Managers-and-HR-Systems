# Great Britain employment: legal orientation

**Status: sourced legal draft, not approved legal advice.** Initial scope: England, Scotland and Wales. Checked 4 November 2026 using the source register. Direct legislation retrieval was incomplete for several provisions; some checks used indexed excerpts or enacted text. Current consolidated wording, commencement, coverage and case-law interpretation require qualified review before authoritative use. Nothing here determines a specific dispute or employment outcome.

## Disability and coverage

The Equality Act definition concerns an impairment's substantial, long-term adverse effect on normal daily activities. Section 6 must be read with Schedule 1, including provisions on long-term effects and certain conditions. A diagnostic label alone does not settle the test. Support can be offered without resolving legal status. [L13](../evidence/source-register.md#l13), [L16](../evidence/source-register.md#l16).

Employment coverage and exceptions depend on status and facts. Section 39 addresses employees/applicants; other work relationships have separate provisions. Do not equate a contract label with a final legal status assessment. [L17](../evidence/source-register.md#l17).

## Adjustments

Section 20 covers disadvantage from practices, physical features and lack of auxiliary aids. Read it with section 21 and the employment schedule, including knowledge provisions. Whether an employer knew or could reasonably have been expected to know about disability and relevant disadvantage can matter; do not turn this into a rule requiring disclosure or a diagnosis. Examine the actual barrier and seek appropriate advice. [L14](../evidence/source-register.md#l14), [L15](../evidence/source-register.md#l15).

Reasonableness is contextual. The employer funds required adjustments, while Access to Work supports eligible additional needs and excludes that employer duty. Practical action should not wait unnecessarily for an external application. [L1](../evidence/source-register.md#l1), [L3](../evidence/source-register.md#l3).

Do not infer automatic liability from an inaccessible app, a disagreement or a single description. Provide access, preserve relevant evidence privately and refer legal conclusions to qualified review.

## Recruitment, flexibility and procedures

Pre-offer health/disability questions are restricted, with specified exceptions including assessment adjustments. Keep questions targeted to a permitted purpose and obtain HR advice if unsure. Do not use access requests as selection evidence. [L6](../evidence/source-register.md#l6).

The flexible-working request procedure and disability-adjustment duties are separate. A disability-related request may engage both; an employer should not process it only as an ordinary preference without considering adjustment obligations. This module does not reproduce request limits or deadlines because these require current-framework verification. [L2](../evidence/source-register.md#l2).

Absence recording and trigger changes are individual options rather than automatic blanket exemptions. Formal procedures need access, preparation and appropriate support. Statutory companion categories and broader adjustment support are different questions. The Acas Code is under review; a consultation is not a replacement Code. [L5](../evidence/source-register.md#l5), [L12](../evidence/source-register.md#l12), [L23](../evidence/source-register.md#l23).

## Data, advice and release checks

For health information, establish necessary purpose, lawful basis, relevant special-category condition and safeguards. Do not assume employee agreement cures the employment power imbalance. See [privacy rules](../SECURITY.md) and [L7](../evidence/source-register.md#l7).

Before authoritative release, a qualified reviewer must verify current law, section 21, all applicable work provisions and exceptions, procedural/accompaniment law, the current flexible-working framework and any relied-on deadlines. Identify reviewer, date, version, scope and unresolved limitations. No deadline for a claim is calculated here; a person facing a dispute should seek timely independent advice.
