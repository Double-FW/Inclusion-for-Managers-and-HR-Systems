# Northern Ireland routing

**Status: limited routing guidance; detailed legal module not released.** The Equality Act 2010 is not the general Northern Ireland employment equality framework. Do not apply the GB module, its procedures or funding route automatically. [Equality laws NI](../evidence/source-register.md#l4).

The Equality Commission NI describes disability protections across recruitment, work terms, training, promotion and dismissal, with adjustments considered for work and interviews. Exact coverage and applicable law require local advice. [Disability discrimination NI](../evidence/source-register.md#l24).

## What to do now

Offer ordinary practical support and interim access while identifying the relevant NI HR/legal adviser. Ask about actual tasks and barriers without a diagnostic assumption. Keep confidential records in approved systems. Use the practical workflows for coordination, but do not describe them as statements of NI legal procedure.

## Unverified matters

Detailed statutory provisions, employment status coverage, flexible-working procedure, accompaniment, tribunal routes/deadlines and local funding remain unverified in this package. Before giving specific legal advice, obtain current NI sources and qualified review. Other jurisdictions and cross-border work also require a separate applicability check.
