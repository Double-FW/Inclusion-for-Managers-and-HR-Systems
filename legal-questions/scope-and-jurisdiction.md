# Scope, jurisdiction and legal advice

**Purpose:** Questions for a context-specific advice conversation. They do not provide a legal conclusion.

## Which jurisdiction and relationship apply?

- Where is the person employed and working, and which jurisdictions may be relevant?
- What is the actual employment or work relationship, rather than only the label in a contract?
- Which current equality, employment, health-and-safety and data-protection provisions apply to this situation?
- Does a cross-border arrangement, contractor relationship or placement need more than one specialist view?

## What needs checking about disability and knowledge?

- Which statutory definition and supplementary provisions are relevant, including long-term or recurring effects?
- What does the employer know, or what might it reasonably be expected to know, about the disability and relevant disadvantage?
- What proportionate functional information or advice would help, and what ordinary support can be provided now?
- Are assumptions about a diagnostic label obscuring the actual work barrier or individual preference?

## How will advice be used?

- What decision is being considered, who is authorised to make it, and what evidence is disputed?
- Are any deadlines, imminent employment decisions or urgent safety/access issues relevant to timely independent advice?
- How will the person understand the decision, correct information and challenge the outcome accessibly?
- Which current consolidated provisions, commencement information and relevant legal interpretation must be verified?

## Preparation and sources

Bring relevant facts, the intended decision, evidence, support already provided and unresolved questions. Check current sources and applicable jurisdiction with the adviser.
