# Recruitment and assessment: legal starting questions

**Purpose:** Questions for a context-specific advice conversation. They do not provide a legal conclusion.

## Role and assessment design

- Which duties are genuinely essential, and could they be performed through a different method or with adjustments?
- Could a neutral-seeming requirement disadvantage candidates, and what evidence supports its necessity and proportionality?
- Do alternative interviews, work samples or trials assess the same relevant competence fairly?
- Does a proposed trial involve productive work, payment or employment-status obligations that need advice?

## Information, adjustments and selection

- At this recruitment stage, are the proposed health/disability questions permitted, necessary and limited to an appropriate purpose?
- What adjustment to the application or assessment would remove the actual disadvantage?
- Are questions, preparation, processing time, communication support and accessible formats provided appropriately?
- Are personality-fit, eye-contact, emotion, social-media or AI inferences introducing unsupported or discriminatory assumptions?

## Evidence and data

- What would justify any aptitude instrument, its accessibility and its use for this role and candidate population?
- How are equality-monitoring data separated from selection, and what assessment evidence needs retaining?
- What transparency, human judgement and challenge arrangements are needed if automated tools are involved?
- Who can review an unfair or inaccessible assessment, and how can the candidate raise a concern without penalty?

## Preparation and sources

Bring relevant facts, the intended decision, evidence, support already provided and unresolved questions. Check current sources and applicable jurisdiction with the adviser.
