# Northern Ireland: questions for a separate advice route

**Purpose:** Questions for a context-specific advice conversation. They do not provide a legal conclusion.

## Identify the applicable framework

- Which current Northern Ireland disability and employment provisions apply to this relationship and stage of employment?
- Which local definitions, coverage, duties and exceptions are relevant, rather than importing the Great Britain framework?
- Does any cross-border element require coordinated advice?

## Adjustments and processes

- What adjustments and interim support should be considered for the actual task or assessment?
- Which local flexible-working, absence, accompaniment, formal-procedure and challenge arrangements apply?
- What local funding or support service is relevant, and what remains the employer’s responsibility?

## Obtain current advice

- What current Equality Commission NI guidance or qualified local advice should be consulted?
- Are any time-sensitive decisions or deadlines relevant, and who will verify them?
- How will access and confidential support continue while detailed legal questions are resolved?

## Preparation and sources

Bring relevant facts, the intended decision, evidence, support already provided and unresolved questions. Check current sources and applicable jurisdiction with the adviser.
