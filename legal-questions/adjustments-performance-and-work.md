# Adjustments, working arrangements and procedures

**Purpose:** Questions for a context-specific advice conversation. They do not provide a legal conclusion.

## The barrier and proposed response

- What practice, physical feature, missing aid or working arrangement produces the relevant disadvantage?
- Which options would address it, including changes to tasks, methods, equipment, environment or support?
- What does reasonableness require in these particular circumstances, and what evidence is needed to assess it?
- What interim access can continue while a decision, specialist advice or funding application is pending?

## Flexibility, resources and funding

- Does the request engage disability-adjustment duties, a flexible-working procedure, contractual change or more than one route?
- Which tasks genuinely require presence or cover, and what shift, break, sequencing or location options remain?
- Which costs are the employer’s responsibility, and which additional support may be eligible under the relevant current scheme?
- If a proposal cannot be agreed, what reasons, alternatives and accessible review route should be provided?

## Performance, absence and return

- Were agreed support, usable instructions, training and tools actually provided before performance was assessed?
- Could disability-related consequences or an inaccessible process affect how attendance, output or conduct evidence should be interpreted?
- What individual recording, leave, trigger, return-to-work or workload arrangements should be considered?
- Are promotion, reward, redeployment, redundancy or exit criteria relying on evidence distorted by barriers?

## Formal meetings and challenge

- Which procedural and accompaniment rights apply to this type of meeting and work relationship?
- Could an interpreter, support worker, advocate, breaks or another response method be required or appropriate beyond ordinary arrangements?
- What notice, material, preparation and communication access will enable a fair response?
- How should harassment, victimisation, safety or a complaint about the manager be addressed promptly and independently?

## Preparation and sources

Bring relevant facts, the intended decision, evidence, support already provided and unresolved questions. Check current sources and applicable jurisdiction with the adviser.
