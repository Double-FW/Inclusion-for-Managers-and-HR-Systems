# Legal questions as starting points

These documents help prepare a conversation with an appropriately qualified adviser. They are questions, not determinations of entitlement, liability, contractual breach, funding or a tribunal outcome. They do not calculate deadlines. Seek timely independent advice where a real dispute or consequential decision is involved.

The sources are carried forward with the review dates and limitations recorded in the original knowledge package. Several statutory reads were limited to excerpts or older views; current consolidated wording, commencement and applicability need checking. Do not treat a linked source as proof that every question has been legally resolved.

## Take useful information, not a speculative diagnosis

Prepare a factual account of the work goal, barrier, effect, existing arrangements, options tried, urgent access needs, proposed decision and disputed evidence. Distinguish documents from recollection and inference. Keep identifiable information in the appropriate confidential system and share only what the adviser needs.

Practical support should continue through appropriate organisational routes while questions are explored. “We need legal advice” should not automatically suspend ordinary assistance or an urgent safe response. [Practical adjustment workflow](../workflows/adjustments.md).
