# Privacy, AI, services and procurement

**Purpose:** Questions for a context-specific advice conversation. They do not provide a legal conclusion.

## Information and confidentiality

- What necessary purpose does each item of employee or applicant information serve?
- Which lawful basis, special-category condition and additional safeguards are relevant to health-related information?
- Does the employment power relationship mean apparent consent is not freely given for the proposed use?
- Who needs access, how long is information needed, and how can the person correct or challenge it?
- Could combinations of small-group or supposedly anonymised details identify someone?

## AI and support technology

- Is the service approved for the data, task, permissions and retention involved?
- Can the person obtain effective non-AI support and stop the trial without consequences?
- Could automated outputs infer health, personality or emotion, reinforce bias or materially affect opportunities?
- What meaningful human review, transparency, access, error correction and challenge routes are needed?
- Who is responsible if an agent takes an unintended action or exposes information?

## Benefits and supplier services

- Can staff access the full benefit, employee-assistance or wellbeing service confidentially and in time?
- What employer, commissioning and supplier duties may be relevant to the specific exclusion?
- What accessibility representations, contract terms, testing evidence and remedial commitments exist?
- Which authorised procurement/legal roles should assess breach, remedies, renewal or payment decisions?
- What independent and confidential interim route remains available while a supplier issue is unresolved?

## Preparation and sources

Bring relevant facts, the intended decision, evidence, support already provided and unresolved questions. Check current sources and applicable jurisdiction with the adviser.
