# A signer needs interpretation

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-03 · **Related guidance:** IE-COM-002

## Situation and barrier

A complex planning meeting offers only automatic captions to a participant who needs signed communication.

## Intended outcome

Participants can follow the full discussion and respond.

## An approach to discuss

Book appropriate qualified interpretation, plan sightlines/content access and test the setup with the participant.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is meeting organiser. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Team sign-language learning or short written exchanges do not substitute for full communication access.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Verify access to contributions, presentation and decisions, not merely interpreter attendance. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
