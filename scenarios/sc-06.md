# Flexibility for a shift worker

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-06 · **Related guidance:** IE-WRK-001

## Situation and barrier

A manager says nurses cannot have flexibility because the job is on site.

## Intended outcome

Working arrangements support sustainable contribution and real job needs.

## An approach to discuss

Examine actual duties, rota, cover, breaks and options; HR identifies adjustment and flexible-working routes.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is line manager. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not promise remote work or exclude the whole occupation.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Review the person’s needs alongside safe cover and sustainable workload. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
