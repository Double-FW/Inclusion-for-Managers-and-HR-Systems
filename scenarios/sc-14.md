# A supplier-payment question

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-14 · **Related guidance:** IE-SYS-003

## Situation and barrier

A team manager proposes refusing payment because a supplier claimed accessibility.

## Intended outcome

Supplier and internal commitments lead to verified fixes.

## An approach to discuss

Preserve defect and contract evidence, maintain staff access and refer remedy/payment decisions to procurement/legal.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is procurement owner. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not threaten payment refusal or declare breach established.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Track agreed repair, verification and unresolved contract decisions separately. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
