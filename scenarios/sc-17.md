# A task timing race

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-17 · **Related guidance:** IE-SYS-002

## Situation and barrier

A manager wants to time one disabled worker against the fastest colleague to set a capability score.

## Intended outcome

Evidence identifies system friction without ranking employees.

## An approach to discuss

Reframe as voluntary system task analysis with context, repeated observations where useful, uncertainty and cumulative burden.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is accessibility lead. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

No employee ranking, forced research or universal delay pass bands.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Test system changes against complete goals and acceptable privacy/effort. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
