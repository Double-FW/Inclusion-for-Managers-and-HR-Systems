# A personality-screening proposal

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-10 · **Related guidance:** IE-REC-003

## Situation and barrier

A hiring team wants to reject applicants with a low culture-fit personality type.

## Intended outcome

Selection does not rely on personality conformity or covert profiling.

## An approach to discuss

Apply the proposed policy against typology-based selection; use a structured relevant work rubric.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is HR. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not claim that all psychometric methods are scientifically invalid or secretly retain fun-test profiles.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Review the criteria and actual evidence; an employer must adopt the policy before it is treated as local authority. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
