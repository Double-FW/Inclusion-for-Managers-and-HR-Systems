# Small-group reporting

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-16 · **Related guidance:** IE-EVA-002

## Situation and barrier

A dashboard would expose two employees’ health categories and promotion outcomes.

## Intended outcome

Learning does not create intrusive employment or disability profiles.

## An approach to discuss

Minimise collection, involve privacy expertise, aggregate/suppress appropriately and separate support from monitoring.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is privacy owner. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not invent a universal anonymity threshold or infer disability from tool use.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Review reidentification risk and usefulness before any data collection or reporting. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
