# An inaccessible wellbeing benefit

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-04 · **Related guidance:** IE-SYS-001

## Situation and barrier

A screen-reader user cannot complete authentication for a mental-health support benefit.

## Intended outcome

People can use essential services with confidentiality and timely access.

## An approach to discuss

The HR commissioning owner and service owner arrange confidential direct access while procurement routes the defect.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is service owner. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not require a colleague to read private wellbeing information or declare automatic discrimination.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Retest authentication and the full service using synthetic data and the relevant interaction method. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
