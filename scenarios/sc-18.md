# A missing upstream source

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-18 · **Related guidance:** IE-EVA-003

## Situation and barrier

An assistant is asked for a detailed rule from an unavailable Double FW repository.

## Intended outcome

Commitments result in funded action and honest public claims.

## An approach to discuss

State that the source was not retrieved, use verified applicable guidance and record the dependency gap.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is executive sponsor. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not invent source contents, assume privacy status or claim the collection was fully audited.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Resolve corrected source/access before asserting its requirements. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
