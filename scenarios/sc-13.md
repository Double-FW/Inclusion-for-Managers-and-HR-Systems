# A Northern Ireland employment query

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-13 · **Related guidance:** IE-ADJ-002

## Situation and barrier

An NI manager asks which Equality Act procedure and Access to Work route applies.

## Intended outcome

An effective adjustment has ownership, implementation and a fair challenge route.

## An approach to discuss

Give the separate NI routing page, maintain practical support and seek current local HR/legal and funding guidance.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is HR. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not apply GB law, procedures or funding automatically.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Use current local advice; no case determination is made here. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
