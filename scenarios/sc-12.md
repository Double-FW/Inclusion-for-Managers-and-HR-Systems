# AI support is ineffective or declined

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-12 · **Related guidance:** IE-AI-001

## Situation and barrier

A summarising tool omits key decisions; the participant wants to stop.

## Intended outcome

People can trial useful support with choice, privacy and a workable alternative.

## An approach to discuss

Stop the trial, restore the accessible non-AI route, record the failure without penalty and review options with the person.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is ai service owner. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not make AI use compulsory or send personnel information to an unapproved service.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Verify retained information, correction and recovery; do not claim observed benefit. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
