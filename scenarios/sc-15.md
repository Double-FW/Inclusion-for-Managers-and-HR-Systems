# An inaccessible team day

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-15 · **Related guidance:** IE-CUL-003

## Situation and barrier

An activity requires independent climbing and the organiser offers compulsory buddying.

## Intended outcome

Teams can improve a process while respecting choice and independence.

## An approach to discuss

Discuss privately, redesign or replace the activity, and offer voluntary assistance alongside equally valued independent participation.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is line manager. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not turn the person’s exclusion into a puzzle or publish their story without permission.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Evaluate choice, status, privacy and all participants’ access. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
