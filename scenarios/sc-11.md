# Return with fluctuating capacity

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-11 · **Related guidance:** IE-PER-002

## Situation and barrier

A phased return has reduced hours but unchanged deliverables and automatic absence triggers.

## Intended outcome

Attendance arrangements and return plans account for individual circumstances.

## An approach to discuss

Discuss sustainable workload, support, individual recording/trigger options, pay information and reviews through HR.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is HR. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not promise all absence is exempt or compress full workload into reduced hours.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Check the real work and support rather than attendance alone. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
