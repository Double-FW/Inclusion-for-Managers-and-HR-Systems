# A Deaf colleague who does not sign

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-02 · **Related guidance:** IE-COM-002

## Situation and barrier

A manager books a sign interpreter without asking a Deaf colleague who relies on captions and text.

## Intended outcome

Participants can follow the full discussion and respond.

## An approach to discuss

Ask privately, arrange the requested usable support and test captions, acoustics and turn-taking.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is meeting organiser. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not prescribe signing from Deaf identity.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Check the full conversation and response route with the colleague, including remote participants. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
