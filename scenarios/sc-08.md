# An adjustment through manager change

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-08 · **Related guidance:** IE-ONB-002

## Situation and barrier

A new manager tells a staff member to reapply for every agreed arrangement.

## Intended outcome

Agreed support survives transitions without unnecessary repeated disclosure.

## An approach to discuss

Plan a private handover with the person and preserve effective support while checking the new work context.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is HR. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not share the whole medical history or automatically withdraw arrangements.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Confirm agreed owners and that access continued during transition. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
