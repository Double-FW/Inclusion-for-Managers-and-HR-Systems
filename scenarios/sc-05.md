# Reduced distraction without a diagnosis

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-05 · **Related guidance:** IE-ADJ-001

## Situation and barrier

An employee asks for a less distracting desk while awaiting an assessment.

## Intended outcome

A person can raise a barrier and receive a clear next step.

## An approach to discuss

Discuss the task and helpful environment, try practical support and name a review owner without waiting for a diagnostic label.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is line manager. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not diagnose or state that legal disability has been proven.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Ask whether concentration, task completion and sustainable effort improved. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
