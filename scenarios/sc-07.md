# Quiet work and voice input

**Type:** Synthetic worked scenario · **Reference:** SC-07 · **Related guidance:** IE-WRK-002

## Situation and barrier

One colleague needs quiet while another relies on voice input in a shared area.

## Intended outcome

People can use the workplace safely and comfortably enough to do their work.

## An approach to discuss

Ask both privately, consider rooms, acoustic changes and scheduling, and trial a workable combination.

## Responsibilities and process

The relevant coordinating role is facilities owner. Involve the person affected and the owners able to change the process, technology or environment. Agree interim access, a next update and a route to review an ineffective response. Keep private details in the appropriate confidential system.

## Limits and alternatives

Do not rank diagnoses, remove someone’s necessary input method or make quiet space physically inaccessible.

## Questions for evaluating the outcome

Check both goals and workloads after the trial. Ask what changed in participation, effort, privacy, independence, confidence or opportunity, and what remains unresolved. An arrangement that improves one dimension may need modification if it creates another barrier.

## Discussion prompts

- What is the essential goal, and which habitual method is creating the barrier?
- What does the affected person want to try or avoid?
- Who can act now, and what requires specialist advice?
- What evidence would show this was useful in the real setting?
